Gruuve Privacy Policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

Gruuve is a workout timer you use on the web at gruuveworkout.com and on Roku TVs. This policy explains what data Gruuve keeps and why.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and Google account ID. We use them to identify your account. We don't get your Google password or access to anything else in your Google account.
  • Your workouts. The workouts you create, including names, exercises, durations and any Spotify links you add.
  • Workout history. When you finish a workout we record the date and how long it took, which powers your stats page.
  • Paired TVs. When you pair a Roku, we store the device's name (as set on the Roku) and a random access token so the TV can load your workouts.
  • A sign-in cookie. The website sets one cookie to keep you signed in. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies.

How we use it

Only to run Gruuve: showing your workouts on your devices and your workout stats. We don't sell your data, show ads, or share your data with anyone for marketing. Workouts you mark as public are visible to other Gruuve users, without your name or email.

Services we rely on

  • Google handles sign-in.
  • Cloudflare hosts the website and stores Gruuve's data.
  • Spotify players appear on the website when a workout has a Spotify link. Spotify's own privacy policy covers those players.

Your choices

  • Delete any workout from the website at any time.
  • Unpair a TV from the website's TV page. Its access token is deleted right away.
  • To delete your account and all of its data, email us at the address below. We'll delete it within 30 days.

Children

Gruuve is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
